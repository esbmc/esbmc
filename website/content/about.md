ESBMC is a joint project with the
[Federal University of Amazonas](https://ufam.edu.br/),
[University of Bristol](http://www.bristol.ac.uk/),
[University of Manchester](http://www.cs.manchester.ac.uk/),
[University of Stellenbosch](http://www.cs.sun.ac.za/), and
[University of Southampton](http://www.ess.ecs.soton.ac.uk/).
